# Hi, I'm Nick

I build and ship products with Claude Code and a team of AI agents. I'm a solo founder at [Supafast](https://supafast.tech).

## What I'm building

- [ToDowl](https://todowl.com): tasks, calendar and notes in one quiet workspace, with Day, 3-day, 4-day and Week views. The calendar is part of Pro.
- [AskAiRank](https://askairank.com): shows how often ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and other AI engines mention your brand, and how you compare with competitors.
- [Linguin](https://linguin.app): an AI translator for web, desktop and mobile.
- [Earthquake Globe](https://earthquakes.site): recent earthquakes on an interactive 3D globe, built on public USGS data.
- [AstroZodify](https://astrozodify.com): daily horoscopes and birth chart insights.
- [Supafast](https://supafast.tech): the studio behind all of the above.

## Open source

Small pieces of how I work, coming to this account:

- [safe-vibe](https://github.com/nickspiz/safe-vibe): guardrail hooks so Claude Code can't wipe prod or force-push. For people who don't read diffs.
- [autonomy-matrix](https://github.com/nickspiz/autonomy-matrix): when your agent should ask and when it should just do it. A CLAUDE.md for non-developers.
- [inbox-to-brain](https://github.com/nickspiz/inbox-to-brain): drop notes in an inbox and the agent files them into your Obsidian vault.
- [say-it-badly](https://github.com/nickspiz/say-it-badly): agent rules for voice dictation. Understand misheard names and numbers, still ask about money, prod and deletions.
- [pilot-first-decisions](https://github.com/nickspiz/pilot-first-decisions): stop paying a chat model for yes/no answers. A decision-model recipe with a benchmark harness.
- [social-post-skills](https://github.com/nickspiz/social-post-skills): one skill per social network, with the real limits and formats, so drafts fit LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Threads, Instagram, Telegram and Reddit.
- [niche-trends-30d](https://github.com/nickspiz/niche-trends-30d): find what worked in your niche over the last 30 days and turn its shape into your own posts, without copying anyone.
